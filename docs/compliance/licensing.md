## Short notice

Only self use is free. Commercial use of any sort needs Orchestrum's prior
approval and a written agreement with Orchestrum Technologies LLP. Agreements:
[sanjeev@orchestrum.in](mailto:sanjeev@orchestrum.in).

This applies to existing and new users alike. Releases published before
21 October 2026 remain available under the Apache License 2.0 they were
released with; versions and updates published from that date are not, so
using them commercially needs an agreement.

## What is free

- Self use for noncommercial purposes: personal study, research, experiments,
  testing and hobby projects with no expected commercial application.
- The other noncommercial uses the licence names, such as use by a charitable
  organisation, educational institution, public research organisation, public
  safety or health organisation, environmental protection organisation or
  government institution.
- Changing the code and sharing it for those purposes, with the licence terms
  and `Required Notice:` lines included.

## What needs a written agreement

Orchestrum treats all of the following as commercial use:

- Internal use by a for-profit business, even if nothing is sold, including
  pilots and proofs of concept.
- Hosting, SaaS or a managed service offered to others.
- Resale, bundling, sublicensing or commercial redistribution, changed or not.
- Embedding Grantex SDKs, libraries, the auth service, the protocol
  implementation or the examples in a commercial product, app or device.
- Consulting, integration, implementation, training or support work for
  clients that uses Grantex.

If you are unsure, treat the use as commercial until Orchestrum confirms
otherwise in writing.

## How to obtain an agreement

Email [sanjeev@orchestrum.in](mailto:sanjeev@orchestrum.in) with your
organisation, the components and versions you want, how you will use them and
who the users are, the expected scale and deployment, and when you need to
start. Orchestrum replies with terms. Commercial use may begin only after a
written agreement is signed by both parties; a request or a reply is not a
licence.

needs an agreement whichever software version you run. "Grantex" and
"AgenticOrg" and their logos are trademarks of Orchestrum Technologies LLP;
commercial use of the names, logos, hosted services or brand, including with
earlier versions, needs prior written approval
([TRADEMARKS.md](https://github.com/mishrasanjeev/grantex/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md)).

Existing hosted customers keep their executed agreements, which control until
they are amended or replaced under their own notice and acceptance terms.
