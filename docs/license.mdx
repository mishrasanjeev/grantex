[sanjeev@orchestrum.in](mailto:sanjeev@orchestrum.in), or see
[commercial licensing](/compliance/licensing) for what to include and how an
agreement is put in place.

## Existing users and earlier releases

Releases published before 21 October 2026, including packages already
published to npm, PyPI and the Go module proxy, remain available under the
Apache License 2.0 they were released with. Versions, updates, fixes and
security patches published from that date are covered by the PolyForm
Noncommercial License 1.0.0, so using them commercially needs an agreement,
whether or not you used an earlier release before.

their URL) and the `Required Notice:` lines at the top of the
