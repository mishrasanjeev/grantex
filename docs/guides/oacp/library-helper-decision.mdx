---
title: OACP Library Helper Decision
description: Why the unmounted C6W4-C6W9 and C6X2 OACP helpers were deleted from Grantex, and what replaces them.
---

Canonical end-to-end flow: [OACP authority overview](./overview).

## Decision

The OACP helpers below lived in `apps/auth-service/src/lib/commerce/oacp-trust-artifacts.ts`
but no route, job or package ever called them. They are deleted, together with their tests.
Grantex no longer describes them as shipped.

| Helper | Former slice | What it did |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `buildOacpC6W4ProtocolAdapterPreview` | C6W4 | Built schema.org, UCP-style, ACP-style, AP2-style, A2A and MCP preview shapes from fixtures. |
| `evaluateOacpC6W5CommitmentBoundaryMetadata` | C6W5 | Classified preview, commitment-adjacent, commitment-bound and blocked actions. |
| `prepareOacpC6W6CommitmentRequestEnvelope` | C6W6 | Prepared non-executing commitment request envelopes. |
| `reconcileOacpC6W7PreparedCommitmentResponse` | C6W7 | Reconciled response evidence with a prepared envelope. |
| `prepareOacpC6W8ExecutionHandoffEligibilityPacket` | C6W8 | Built eligibility and audit-readiness packets. |
| `verifyOacpC6X2CachedArtifactEnvelope` and `verifyOacpC6W9ExecutionControllerHandoffDryRun` | C6X2, C6W9 | Checked cached artifact metadata and dry-run packets. The cache verifier trusted a caller-supplied `signature_verified` flag instead of checking a signature. |

## What replaces them

- Artifact verification is the ES256 detached JWS verifier (`verifyOacpArtifact` in
  `lib/commerce/oacp-jws.ts`), described in [OACP artifact authority](./artifact-authority).
  It checks the signature against the published JWKS and the payload hash, then freshness,
  scope, safety and the OACP Token Status List.
- Protocol mappings stay in the `protocol_adapter` artifact's `adapter_mapping_profile`,
  which AgenticOrg renders from verified cached artifacts.
- Commitment, reconciliation and execution decisions are AgenticOrg's deterministic arbiter
  (a later phase of this programme), not a Grantex library.

## Alternative considered

Mount the helpers behind the internal authority route
(`POST /v1/commerce/oacp/c6z/authority-requests`, same service-token auth) so AgenticOrg
could request adapter previews or eligibility packets from Grantex. Rejected for now:

- none of them took a signed artifact as input; they worked over unsigned fixtures, so
  mounting them would publish output that looks authoritative without being verifiable;
- the UCP and ACP surfaces planned next are rewritten to the published specifications, and
  the C6W4 shapes are not those shapes, so no later work needs them.

If a later surface needs an artifact-derived adapter from Grantex, it is built over
verified artifacts and mounted behind the authority route, with its own tests.
