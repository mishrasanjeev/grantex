selling its own goods, and using agent outputs in your business. Revenue from

between conducting your own business and selling platform or agent-service access
to other parties.

## 3. Commercialization agreement

Before reselling, sublicensing, commercially hosting, white-labeling or embedding
Covered Software or its platform capabilities in a paid product or agent service
offered to third parties, obtain a separate written agreement with Orchestrum
Technologies LLP.

An agreement signed by an authorized Orchestrum representative must identify its
permitted offering and scope. An email inquiry, a paid infrastructure account or
a customer subscription does not itself grant reseller or OEM rights.
The applicable signed commercial agreement governs its covered commercial use.

Providing implementation or consulting services for a customer's own permitted
deployment is not automatically platform resale. Selling hosted platform access
or a platform-based agent service as part of the offering is commercialization.
Confirm borderline cases at sanjeev@orchestrum.in before relying on this license.

Distribution as part of a paid platform, paid hosted service or paid embedded
