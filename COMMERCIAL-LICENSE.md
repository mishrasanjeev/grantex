Any commercial use, whatever its size and whether or not money changes hands.

- **Internal use by a for-profit business**, even if nothing is sold: running

  customers or operations, including pilots and proofs of concept.
- **Hosting, SaaS and managed services**: offering Grantex, or a service built
  on it, to other people or organisations, on your infrastructure or anyone

- **Resale and distribution**: selling, licensing, bundling or redistributing

- **Embedding**: including Grantex SDKs, libraries, the auth service, the

- **Consulting and services**: using Grantex to deliver paid consulting,

If you are not sure whether your use is commercial, treat it as commercial
until Orchestrum confirms otherwise in writing.
